    Common questions

    Who can vote?

    Any enrolled PCU student with a verified account.

    How do I get verified?

    Open your account menu, choose “Verify now”, and submit your student details. You will be notified once it is approved.

    Can I change my vote?

    No. Once you submit your ballot it is final, so review your choices first.

    When will I see the results?

    Results are posted on the Results page after voting ends on April 10.
